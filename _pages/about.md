My name is Gabriela Kadlecová and I am a Machine Learning Researcher at [Distil Labs](https://distillabs.ai).

I have a PhD in Neural Architecture Search from Charles University in Prague. I also did an internship at Amazon Alexa in Turin.

In my research, I focused on Neural architecture search, specifically on performance prediction, zero-cost proxies and graph neural network used as surrogate models. I also worked on the open-source library [whittle](https://github.com/whittle-org/whittle) for LLM compression.
My current research focuses on small language models and knowledge distillation -- trying to push the limits of small-scale tool calling!
